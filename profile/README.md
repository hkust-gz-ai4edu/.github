## 让真实实验，成为看得见的学习。

**AI4Edu 是香港科技大学（广州）的跨学科项目团队。** 我们连接实验教学、人工智能、软件工程与用户研究，围绕 **LabMate AI** 探索真实操作中的学习支持。

一堂实验课里，每张实验台都处于不同的进度。我们希望把学生平板、眼镜与教师工作台连接起来：帮助学生做好准备，在操作中获得与进度相符的提示，让教师及时看见需要帮助的工位，并在课后用关键事件复盘学习过程。

AI4Edu is an interdisciplinary project team at **The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)**. We bring together laboratory teaching, AI, software engineering and user research to develop **LabMate AI**, a platform for learning through real laboratory practice.

Our goal is to connect student tablets, wearable interfaces and a teacher workspace across preparation, practice and reflection—making learning progress visible and keeping instructors involved when judgment is needed.

## LabMate AI · 我们正在构建什么

| 学习阶段 · Learning stage | 产品方向 · Product direction |
| --- | --- |
| **实验前 · Prepare** | 流程预习、器材知识与计算推导，帮助学生带着理解进入实验。<br>Protocol preparation, equipment knowledge and calculation practice. |
| **实验中 · Practice** | 结合动作与流程状态提供提示，将低置信度或冲突情况交给教师判断。<br>Contextual guidance from actions and workflow states, with uncertain cases escalated to instructors. |
| **实验后 · Reflect** | 用关键事件、纠正与恢复路径，支持学生复盘和教师反馈。<br>Review key events, corrections and recovery paths to support reflection and feedback. |

项目目前处于**研发与原型验证阶段**。官网呈现产品原型与设计；真实课堂中的可靠性、教学效果和规模化部署仍需验证。

The project is in **research and prototype development**. The website presents product prototypes and designs; classroom reliability, educational outcomes and deployment at scale remain to be validated.

## 研究与开发 · Research & development

- **感知与流程理解 · Perception & workflow understanding** — 物体与手部感知、动作识别、时序建模与实验流程状态。 Object and hand perception, action recognition, temporal modeling and protocol states.
- **多端教学体验 · Learning interfaces & systems** — 学生平板、眼镜协同、教师工作台与关键事件复盘。 Student tablets, wearable interaction, teacher workspaces and event-based reflection.
- **课程共创与用户研究 · Course co-design & user research** — 与学生和教师共同明确真实需求、试用范围与评价依据。 Work with students and instructors to define needs, trial scope and evaluation criteria.
